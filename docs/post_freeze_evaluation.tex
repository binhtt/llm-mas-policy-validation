\subsection{Post-Freeze Integration Assessment}
\label{sec:post-freeze-assessment}

We evaluated the archived four-agent configuration on four scenario
families written after its policies had been frozen. The bundle reuses
the individual freshness, reservation, retry, and partial-fill policies
verbatim. Its Reconciliation policy applies the reported fill quantity
for each unseen notification, including valid notifications delivered
after cancellation.

The scenarios exercise retry before and after exchange acceptance,
late partial fills with delayed duplicate delivery, the cash boundary
under pending commitments, and the quote-age and order-quantity
boundaries. Each family runs with 30 seeds that vary the input prices
or quantities within its operation sequence. Scenario checkpoints
specify expected exchange orders and ledger balances. We compare
syntax and command-schema checks with the same checks augmented by
behavioral monitors and scenario checkpoints, using identical executions
for both conditions.

The frozen configuration passed all 120 executions. Nine single-edit
policy variants compiled and emitted schema-valid commands in all
1,080 variant executions. The behavioral checks flagged 330 executions
and detected all nine variants, with detection defined as at least one
reported violation per variant. These results characterize the
selected faults and scenarios in the FIFO simulator. The scenarios
were written with access to the frozen policy source, and the variants
were seeded manually.

For comparison, we ran the same frozen configuration and nine variants
on the 23 original extended scenario families with 30 seeds per family.
The frozen configuration passed all 690 executions. The original suite
flagged 1,290 of 6,210 variant executions and detected all nine variants.
Both suites detected the same variants. The post-freeze suite provides
additional evidence from different event sequences and explicit checkpoints.
The suites used different numbers of scenario families and retained their
own inputs and assertions.
